Aedwon, for production Everything approved, in one place. The boards beside this are the reference build; this sheet is the numbers. Design size is 1440 × 900.

The idea

One room. The work hangs on the wall (Wall, or the same projects as a list, Index). Below it, on the same wall, a shelf of six volumes is About; the sixth is where people write to you (Contact). A project opens in front of the wall as an open book: details on the left page, the work and its story on the right. Back returns you to the exact spot on the wall.

Routes

/Wall. Drag, scroll or trackpad moves along it; the tick rail on the right jumps to any project.
/indexThe same projects as a list, plus the writing (Index only). Filter by type, sort by Project (A–Z) or Type (grouped), type to find, ↑ ↓ to move. With Writing chosen the third column is TOPIC.
/writing/:slugA post, opened exactly like a project. Back returns to the Index.
/work/:slugA project page, opened over the wall. ← → move between projects. Esc or Back returns to the wall.
/aboutThe shelf. Each volume opens into a book with turning pages.
/contactThe shelf, with Vol. 06 pulled out and open.

Motion

Wall ↔ IndexFold, 1.5s. Tiles become the rows' thumbnails. Going to Index, the frozen header fades in only once rows have landed (0.45s); going back it fades out first (0.38s, drifts up 8px), then rows fold.
Sort / filter / findSwap: rows that keep their place don't move; rows that change fade out 0.10s and back in 0.22s at their new place, 18ms apart, rising 6px.
Type filterIcons stay put; a 1.5px line slides under the chosen icon (0.5s, cubic-bezier(.34,1.3,.64,1)); its name swaps in one fixed spot.
Row previewPanel fades in beside the list (spring k120 d20), glides row to row, the picture crossfades. Arrow appears only on the hovered or focused row.
Wall → AboutRoom camera, spring k48 d14. The wall tips back (up to 46°), slides up 55% of 740px and dissolves at its lower edge; the shelf rises 740px. Scrolling past the last project peeks the spines (up to 35%), a further 700px of push commits; scrolling up from the shelf reverses it.
Project openPage fades in and rises 26px (0.55s, smoothstep) while the wall steps back to 96.5% and fades; closing 0.42s. Wall scroll position is kept.
BooksOpen 1.9s; page turn 0.95s (0.55s when skipping); six-strip curl; settle crossfade 0.16s.
Points30px grid, 1px points at 7.5%; within 200px of the cursor they brighten to 40% and lean away up to 5px; drift with the wall at 0.55×; rest at 25% while a project is open; hidden under the Index header.
Phone wallVertical picker: snap scrolling, 180px per project; each card sits on a drum (30° apart, radius 390px), shrinking 8% and fading 50% per step; only the chosen one and its neighbours show.
Phone booksStack of six, spines out. Opening: slide out 14px (0.14s), grow to reading size (0.5s), cover opens (1.05s). Reading: a spread with the camera on one page; pan between pages 0.45s; a turn is the desktop curl (six strips, 0.9s) with the camera carried across by the page. Keyboard: controls fade, book lifts so the field clears the keyboard by 18px.
Reduced motionEvery transition jumps to its end state; the points stay still.

Project pages

Left page (fixed)Back · Title · Description · Stack logos (named on hover) · details as icon + fact (platform = type icon, status = dot, role, when, scale, client…) · main link · previous / next.
Right page (scrolls)The work, then the story. Depth per project: note (work only), writeup (The brief / What I built / How it went), case study (the whole thing on the page; Pantas).
ContentsTicks at the right edge, one per section; the current one is longer. Hover the edge to open the names; click to jump. Only when there's more than one section.
