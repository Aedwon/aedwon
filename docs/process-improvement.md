# Process improvement

This file holds this project's active written rules and required steps under the global process-improvement process. Every agent reads it at the start of a session. When Aedwon rejects or corrects work, write the entry in [`docs/process-improvement-archive.md`](process-improvement-archive.md) in the same turn, before anything else continues. Rules and steps together stay at 12 or fewer.

Design taste lives in `docs/design-principles.md`. Read it before any design work.

## Written rules

1. Deliver designs in Claude Design, not as loose HTML files. Refine in local HTML first, where a render can be viewed, then move the result to the canvas. His words (Nov 17) were "I would prefer things to be in Claude Design, not in html".
2. Avoid overused fonts such as Geist, Inter and Instrument Serif. His words (Nov 17) were "others will spot the overused fonts".

## Required steps

1. **Lock shape and facts first.** Before drafting repeated copy or drawing a diagram, confirm one part's structure and every connection in plain sentences. Keep his approved decisions in the spec and check each revision against them.
2. **Count visitor words.** State a budget (60 words per part by default, leaving out prompts, quotes and builder notes) and report the count before showing copy.
3. **Show, do not describe.** A look or motion proposal is a live sample he can see, viewed and critiqued by the agent first. After a rejection, collect what he dislikes before building again.
4. **Check before showing.** Run the design check, fix its errors, and review once against `docs/design-principles.md`.
