## The "Unslop" Rules for Aedwon's Copy

1. **First-person & Plain English:**
   - Say: *"I'm Aedwon. I build web apps, offline tools, and community bots."*
   - Never say: *"Independent builder & digital artisan crafting bespoke experiences."*

2. **Lead with What & Why, not Buzzwords:**
   - Say: *"QR Studio — A customizable QR code generator where all image rendering happens client-side in the browser. No tracking, no backend, no server uploads."*
   - Never say: *"An innovative, robust, privacy-first paradigm leveraging cutting-edge canvas rendering."*

3. **Concrete Numbers & Real Constraints:**
   - State real facts: `170,000+ members moderated across 6 servers`, `4 custom Discord bots in production`, `Android TV kiosk running 8 hours offline without signal`.
   - Never hide behind vague abstractions like "vast community reach" or "scalable cloud infrastructure".

4. **Zero Agency Slop:**
   - No sales tiers, no "The stranger sale vs. The regular sale", no fake consultative jargon ("two-week diagnostic package").
   - Honest availability: *"If you want to talk about a project, get help with community tooling, or just say hi, shoot me an email or find me on Discord."*
